## 生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Create one finished app icon for LocalShare. This must be a PURE GRAPHIC SYMBOL, absolutely no character, face, eyes, mouth or mascot. The brand needs the immediate recognition of a great music/social app icon, but with a wholly original utility symbol.
Design: a single bold, beautifully drawn WHITE PAPERCLIP SYMBOL, tilted diagonally from lower-left to upper-right. One continuous smooth substantial stroke creates the long outer loop and shorter inner return. Make the proportions compact and powerful, with an oversized open counter and optically precise rounded bends. Give the two open stroke endpoints small clean 45-degree cuts, providing a subtle sense of movement and a distinctive signature. It must be instantly recognizable as an elegant paperclip rather than an abstract letter or chain link. The symbol represents attaching, collecting and passing information.
Background: cheerful saturated jade-teal green, approximately #14B89A, completely solid flat color. Symbol: crisp clean warm white #FFFDF7. Only these two colors. Flat vector-like brand graphic, smooth crisp edges. No dimensionality whatsoever.
Composition: center the diagonal mark optically, with a compact total footprint around 58-62% of canvas width and 60-65% of height, plenty of green negative space. The square image is fully opaque, its green background extending to all four corners.
Strict avoid list: any face or creature, eyes or mouth, spooky or ghost-like shape, document with expression, black background, gloomy colors, lettering, monograms, 3D, gradients, shadows, lighting, material texture, bevels, glass, folded ribbons, intertwined cards, solid square L shape, music note, headphones, speech bubble, phone, cables, arrow decorations, tiny details, outer rounded-square mask, framing, mockups, comparison grids, watermarks. Just the finished two-color SYMBOL icon.
